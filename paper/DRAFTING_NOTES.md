## 5. Remaining limitations, ranked

1. **The daily–monthly reversal is unexplained.** It is the principal limitation, and resolving
   it needs intraday or announcement-level evidence, which would be a new phase.
2. **The correlation change is statistically uncertain.** Its inference is post hoc and assumes
   independence across periods.
3. **Intervals ignore date selection.** For periods bounded by the data-selected 2004 and 2017
   dates, intervals are too narrow.
4. **Some reference support is indirect.** Three references rest on working-paper or secondary
   abstracts, and no full text was read.
5. **BOJ event classes are absent from public results.**
6. **H1b numbers were quoted, not re-run** this session.
7. **Untested hypotheses remain.** Shock composition and observation timing are discussed but not
   tested.
